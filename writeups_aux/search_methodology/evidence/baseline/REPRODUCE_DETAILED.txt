The research scripts read Data and write research outputs. They are not a ready-to-
upload inference package. A final submission needs package-local feature helpers,
model-only frozen artifact reads, exact dependency pins, and the required
InferenceModel interface. Frozen models stored here were trained only through 2021
for the 2022 audit. A final full-public-data retrain was NOT performed.
